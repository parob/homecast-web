Homecast Analytics records sensor readings so you can see what happened overnight, compare rooms or check a battery that's running low.

![A small wireless sensor on a window sill beside lightly misted glass](/blog/apple-home-history-and-analytics/window.webp)

## Turn on recording

An admin can enable recording under **Settings → Homes → your home → Analytics**. It's off by default. Readings start from the moment you enable it.

Open **Analytics** from the dashboard's **⋯** menu and choose a room. It's also available from an accessory's right-click menu or expanded panel.

![A room in Analytics with lighting activity and temperature charts](/blog/apple-home-history-and-analytics/room.webp "A room's lighting and temperature, shown with example data.")

## Compare readings

For a cold bedroom, compare a few nights with another room. Turn on **Targets** to show thermostat settings, then hover over the charts to compare the same moment. Some sensors update less often than others.

Bookmark a view to come back to it, or ask a [connected assistant](/blog/control-apple-home-from-claude-and-chatgpt/), “What was the bedroom temperature overnight?”

<details>
<summary>Storage, exports and sharing history</summary>

Analytics is available on every plan. History stays on your Mac in Community Edition, or in your Cloud account. Turning recording off keeps existing data; **Export CSV** and **Delete data…** are in the home's Analytics settings.

In Community Edition, storage above 500 MB triggers removal of the oldest raw readings; hourly and daily summaries remain.

Guest links only show history if you enable **Analytics on shared links** beneath recording. It covers the shared devices, with no export or deletion access. Home members keep their normal access.

See the [Analytics guide](https://docs.homecast.cloud/guides/history) for the storage details.

</details>
