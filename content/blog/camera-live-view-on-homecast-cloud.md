You can now check Apple Home cameras from the Homecast dashboard on Android or in a browser.

You'll need a **Cloud Managed** relay; your own Mac isn't supported yet. Enable **Show cameras** under **Settings → Homes → your home → Cameras**. It's off by default.

Each tile shows a still with its age in the corner. Tap for live view: a few frames a second, labelled **Live · No audio**.

If live view is unavailable, the still refreshes roughly every ten seconds. Audio and recordings need your usual camera app. To share camera access, use a [member account](/blog/your-home-shouldnt-care-which-phone-you-own/); guest links don't include cameras.

<details>
<summary>Refresh times</summary>

Visible tiles refresh about once a minute for mains-powered cameras and every ten minutes for battery cameras. Doorbells can use a portrait tile.

Live-view capacity is limited, so you may need to wait for a stream.

</details>
